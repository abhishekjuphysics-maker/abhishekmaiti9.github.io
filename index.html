About

I am an experimental condensed-matter physicist working on quantum materials, nanoscale electronic phenomena, and scanning probe microscopy. I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Radboud University, The Netherlands. Previously, I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at Université Paris-Saclay / CNRS, France.

I received my PhD in Physics from Jadavpur University, India, based on research conducted at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science.

Research interests

My research interests include:

  • Atomic-scale quantum sensing and shot-noise microscopy
  • Artificial quantum materials and atomic-scale engineering
  • Quantum states in superconducting and topological materials
  • Atomic-scale neuromorphic matter
  • Chiral interfaces and spin-dependent transport
  • Nanoscale optoelectronics and metal-halide perovskites
