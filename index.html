About

Recent advances in condensed matter physics and material science are increasingly shaped by two major frontiers: nanoscience and nanotechnology, and quantum materials. My research aims to bridge these fields by harnessing nanoscience to probe and engineer quantum materials with atomic precision, uncovering and controlling coherent quantum functionalities and emergent correlations. I am interested in understanding how quantum phenomena emerge, how individual quantum states behave and interact, and how we can ultimately manipulate and control them. To explore these, I use scanning probe microscopy and related techniques to visualize, probe, and manipulate quantum systems with atomic-scale precision. My long-term vision is to build quantum matter and technologies atom by atom, using individual quantum building blocks to create functionalities with long coherence time.

Research Interests

  • Atomic-Scale Quantum Sensing: Probing local quantum phenomena, electronic correlations, and atomic-scale interactions using advanced scanning probe techniques.
  • Atomic-Scale Neuromorphic Matter: Exploring collective atomic and electronic processes for adaptive, memory-bearing, and brain-inspired nanoscale matter.
  • Artificial Quantum Materials: Designing and investigating emergent quantum states through atomic-scale structural and interfacial engineering.
  • Chiral Interfaces and Quantum Spintronics: Investigating spin-dependent interactions and chiral effects at engineered interfaces.
  • Nanoscale Optoelectronics: Understanding charge-carrier dynamics, recombination, and spin-dependent phenomena in metal-halide perovskites.
